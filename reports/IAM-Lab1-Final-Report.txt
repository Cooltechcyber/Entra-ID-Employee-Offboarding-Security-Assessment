1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

An identity and access management (IAM) assessment was conducted for Harborview Accounting Services in a simulated Microsoft Entra ID environment.

The assessment identified an employee offboarding control failure involving David Miller, a former employee whose user account remained enabled and retained membership in two security groups: SG-Accounting-Staff and SG-Finance-Documents.

The initial security risk was assessed as High (provisional) because the account could potentially have been used to access organizational resources if valid credentials and permissions were available. No unauthorized access or data exposure was established during the investigation.

Remediation included disabling the account, revoking sign-in sessions, and removing both identified security group memberships. Microsoft Entra audit records and subsequent account checks supported successful completion of these actions.

The residual risk associated with the verified access paths was assessed as Low (provisional). Other potential access paths and the underlying organizational root cause were not fully assessed.

The assessment recommends formal offboarding procedures, lifecycle automation, periodic access reviews, and exception monitoring to reduce the likelihood of similar failures.

3. SECURITY FINDINGS

FINDING IAM-001: INCOMPLETE EMPLOYEE OFFBOARDING

Severity: High (provisional)
Affected User: David Miller
Department: Accounting

Description:
The assessment identified a simulated former employee whose Microsoft Entra ID account remained enabled after departure.

The account also retained membership in the following security groups:
- SG-Accounting-Staff
- SG-Finance-Documents

Security Risk:
An enabled former employee account may permit unauthorized authentication if valid credentials remain available. Retained security group memberships may also provide access to organizational resources where permissions have been configured.

Evidence:
- Account enabled = Yes during initial investigation.
- Both identified security group memberships were present.
- No sign-in records were found during the reviewed investigation.

Impact:
Potential unauthorized access to business systems or information. Actual unauthorized access and data exposure were not demonstrated.

Finding Status: Remediated and verified for the identified account and group memberships.

7. SECURITY RECOMMENDATIONS

RECOMMENDATION 1: FORMAL EMPLOYEE OFFBOARDING PROCEDURE
Priority: High
Establish a documented offboarding process requiring authorized HR or management notification, timely account disablement, session revocation, access removal, and verification of completion.

RECOMMENDATION 2: AUTOMATED IDENTITY LIFECYCLE MANAGEMENT
Priority: High
Implement an approved workflow integrating employee termination notifications with Microsoft Entra ID account and access management. Include error handling, exception notifications, and completion records.

RECOMMENDATION 3: PERIODIC USER ACCESS REVIEWS
Priority: High
Conduct recurring reviews of active accounts and security group memberships. Require managers or resource owners to confirm continued business need and remove unauthorized access.

RECOMMENDATION 4: OFFBOARDING MONITORING AND ALERTS
Priority: High
Monitor offboarding completion against verified termination records. Alert IAM or IT Operations personnel when accounts remain enabled or required access removal actions fail.

IMPLEMENTATION CONSIDERATIONS
Assign accountable control owners, establish measurable completion requirements, test workflows before production deployment, and retain audit evidence.

Recommendation Status: Proposed. These organizational controls were not implemented or tested during this lab.

Affected User: David Miller

CONFIRMED TECHNICAL CONDITION
The simulated former employee's Microsoft Entra ID account remained enabled and retained membership in SG-Accounting-Staff and SG-Finance-Documents.

- Missing or ineffective HR-to-IT offboarding notification.
- Incomplete manual account deprovisioning procedures.

- Inadequate periodic review of active accounts and group memberships.

The underlying organizational root cause could not be confirmed using the available Microsoft Entra ID evidence. The potential contributing factors listed above are hypotheses requiring further investigation.

RECOMMENDED FOLLOW-UP
Review HR termination notifications, IT service requests, offboarding procedures, ownership assignments, and historical completion records to determine where the process failed.

Root Cause Status: Unconfirmed — Further organizational investigation required.

Four organizational security improvements were recommended but not implemented during this assessment.
