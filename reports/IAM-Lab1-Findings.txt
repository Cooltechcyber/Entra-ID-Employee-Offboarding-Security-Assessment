IAM LAB 1 — SECURITY RECOMMENDATION 016
Client: Harborview Accounting Services

Recommendation: Establish a formal employee offboarding procedure.

Risk addressed: Former employee accounts remaining enabled with unnecessary access after employment ends.

Recommended control: Require Human Resources or an authorized manager to notify IT of employee departures through a documented offboarding request.

Required IT actions:
- Disable the departing employee's account at the authorized termination time.
- Revoke active sign-in sessions.
- Remove unnecessary security group memberships and application access.
- Verify that access removal was successful.
- Retain audit evidence and document completion.

Suggested control owner: IT Operations / Identity and Access Management

Priority: High
Implementation status: Recommended — not implemented or tested in this lab.
